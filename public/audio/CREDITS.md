All loops below are CC0 (public domain) — no attribution required, credited here anyway.

- `thunder.mp3` — from ["100 CC0 SFX #2"](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2) (`sfx100v2_thunder_01`), OpenGameArt.org, CC0.
- `keys.mp3` — from ["typing soundeffect"](https://opengameart.org/content/typing-soundeffect), OpenGameArt.org, CC0.
- `cafe.mp3` — from ["coffee shop ambience"](https://freesound.org/people/waweee/sounds/370973/) by waweee, Freesound, CC0.

All six were re-encoded to 128kbps MP3 to keep the repo small.

`cafe.mp3` is a 45-second seamless loop cut from a 4:56 recording. The source window was
picked by measuring per-second RMS and taking the steadiest 48 seconds, so no single
event (a laugh, a clatter) repeats audibly on every pass. Its final 3 seconds were then
crossfaded over its first 3 seconds to close the loop, and the result normalised to
-28 dB mean so it sits alongside `rain.mp3`.
